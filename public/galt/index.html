Exact buyer

This sprint is narrow by design.

Apply when the current workflow already consumes enough senior time to repay a bounded implementation. This is not a general AI transformation engagement.

Strong fit

  • Company: 5–25-person B2B agency or consultancy
  • Revenue: $1M–$10M annually
  • Volume: at least 10 qualified opportunities each month
  • Contract value: above $15,000 on average
  • Current burden: at least $4,000 per month of senior-team time
  • Stack: Gmail or forms, CRM or Notion, and Google Docs
  • Authority: able to buy within one week

Not for this sprint

  • Pre-revenue companies
  • Regulated healthcare or other sensitive regulated data
  • Enterprise procurement cycles
  • Low-ticket agencies without measurable proposal burden
  • Requests spanning several workflows or business units
  • Autonomous external sending without a human gate
